Interpretation
--------------
The LLM successfully supported hierarchical RAG orchestration and improved
explainability by identifying available, reputable, privacy-compatible clients
and explaining each selection. It did not act as the training model or directly
replace FedAvg aggregation.

This was a 5-client/3-round pilot run, not a full 10-client/10-round paper
experiment. The 63.47% pilot accuracy should not be compared directly with the
reported full-run accuracy values. Pilot dropouts and limited availability also
prevented a reliable measurement of the LLM's effect on final accuracy.
